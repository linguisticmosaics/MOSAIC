Forty years ago, a twenty-three year old was the first to cross la finish line at the Boston Marathon. She finished the race with the third-fastest time ever recorded for a mujer in the history of the event. Most of the runners were wet either from sweat or from pouring cups of agua on their heads to cool down or both. When the organizers approached her to say congratulations, they were surprised porque she was completely dry. Everyone was confused pero then some of the people who were watching told them what had really happened. They said they had seen the woman appear in the last half mile, quickly emerging from the crowd with a swift paso into the road. She sprinted to the finish line from there. The organizer in charge had never in his vida seen anyone cheat like this. He took her title away y gave it to the real winner who was another young woman of about the same age. It was later discovered que the same woman had also cheated in other events including three meses earlier in the New York Marathon, where she had taken the subway!
